The Chapters of K.V.T

My name is Kier VaSean Turpin, and every chapter of my life has carried a different name, a different energy, and a different purpose.

Back when I played basketball in the military, people knew me as Penny. It was a time of discipline, teamwork, and learning how to lead both on and off the court.

While I was still in the military, another part of me started to grow: dance. It became my ultimate form of expression and a way to break free from the rigid structures of daily life.

Later, when I stepped into gaming and streaming, a new name emerged: Meahgee. In the digital world, I built communities, connected with people globally, and learned the power of technology to bring us together.

Today, I’m stepping fully into myself as Kier, and building my future under the name K.V.T.

Not just initials — but an acronym that represents my mission:
Knowledge. Versatility. Tech.
Everything I create, release, or build for the world will live under that name.

I recently earned my Bachelor’s in Information Technology from the University of Arizona Global Campus, graduating Magna Cum Laude. This academic journey formalized the tech skills I’ve been building for years.

I’m the founder of K.V.Tech and the creator of MoodMix, an app designed to bridge the gap between emotional processing and music discovery.

My creative identity is shaped by my heritage and history, deeply rooted in the resilience and innovation of my ancestors. I am dedicated to reclaiming that legacy.

Outside of tech, I’m passionate about broadcasting, audio engineering, and live production.

Right now, I’m focused on advocacy, using my platforms to support veterans, creatives, and marginalized communities, ensuring everyone has the tools they need to succeed.

Every chapter — Penny, the dancer, Meahgee, Kier, and now K.V.T. — has shaped the person I am today.

"And everything I build is rooted in one mission:
Create with purpose. Lead with empathy. Leave things better than I found them."
