Why I Built This App

I built MoodMix for the people who struggle to express themselves out loud — not because they don’t feel deeply, but because sometimes the words just don’t come out right...

It’s a space where you can be honest without judgment... rooted in therapeutic advice I received about the power of words, journaling, and the truth that music has also played a role in saving my life.

What started as a project during my Mobile Application Design & Development class became something much more personal and real. I wanted to build something pure and honest for the heart, soul, and mind.
