Why I Built This App

I built MoodMix for the people who struggle to express themselves out loud — not because they don’t feel deeply, but because sometimes the words just don’t come out right...

It’s a space where you can be honest without judgment, where you can release what you’re holding, and where music helps say the things you can’t always say yourself or others...

What started as a project during my Mobile Application Design & Development class... became something much more personal and real...
