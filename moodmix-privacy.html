Privacy Policy for MoodMix

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Developer: Kier Turpin (KVT)

MoodMix ("the Application") is provided by Kier Turpin (KVT) as an independent music discovery and playback application. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding policies and procedures concerning data handling when using MoodMix.

1. Information Collection and Personal Data

MoodMix does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). No account registration, email address, user credentials, phone numbers, or payment details are collected or stored.

2. Local Device Audio & Media Permissions

The Application requests access to read audio files stored locally on your device (via standard Android media permissions, including READ_MEDIA_AUDIO) solely to allow playback of your downloaded music files:

  • On-Device Processing: All file scanning, track metadata reading, and audio decoding occur entirely locally on your physical device.
  • Zero Uploads: MoodMix never transmits, uploads, syncs, or shares your personal audio files or library structure with any external servers.

3. Third-Party Music Services (Jamendo & Audius)

MoodMix interfaces with public API endpoints provided by Jamendo and Audius to discover and stream open-catalog, royalty-free music and fetch album artwork. When streaming music or querying tracks via these platforms, standard network requests (including IP address and standard HTTP request headers) are handled directly by their respective network nodes.

Their data and network policies can be reviewed at:

  • Jamendo Privacy Policy
  • Audius Privacy Policy

4. Local Device Storage & Caching

The Application uses standard local device storage solely to maintain seamless performance:

  • Playback State & Queue: Saved locally on the device using Android persistence to preserve your queue and preferences across app restarts.
  • Audio Buffering: Temporary on-device playback caching handled by Jetpack Media3 / ExoPlayer for smooth playback.

5. Analytics and Tracking

MoodMix does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral user trackers, or telemetry monitoring services.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not target individuals under the age of 13. MoodMix does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published directly to this page with a revised effective date.
