Coffee Origins

Discover where coffee comes from and how each region shapes its flavor.

Coffee has traveled the world for centuries, evolving through culture, climate, and tradition. From the ancient Ethiopian highlands to the volcanic soils of Central America, each region brings its own story — and its own flavor profile — to every cup.

Major Coffee Regions

Ethiopia

Floral, fruity, and tea-like. The birthplace of coffee with ancient traditions.

Latin America

Balanced, bright, and sweet. Known for chocolate and citrus notes.

East Africa

Vibrant, wine-like acidity with berry and floral characteristics.

Asia-Pacific

Earthy, deep, and bold. Often low-acidity with herbal or spicy notes.

Processing Methods

Washed, natural, and honey processes dramatically change flavor and texture.

Elevation

Higher altitudes produce denser beans with more complex flavor potential.
